DESTINATION 01 / EARTH'S MOON

THE MOON:
A SILENT ARCHIVE.

The Apollo missions left behind equipment, experiments, and traces of human exploration. These objects remain on the lunar surface, preserving a record of our first journeys to another world.

01 / APOLLO LEGACY

WHAT REMAINS
ON THE MOON?

Many objects were left because the missions were designed to bring astronauts home, not to return every piece of equipment.

LUNAR OBJECT / 001
◈

LUNAR MODULE DESCENT STAGES

The lower stages of Apollo Lunar Modules helped astronauts land on the Moon. The ascent stages carried the astronauts back to lunar orbit, leaving the descent stages on the surface.

APOLLO MISSIONS
LUNAR OBJECT / 002
▤

LUNAR ROVING VEHICLES

Three Apollo missions used battery-powered rovers to travel farther across the lunar surface. The vehicles were left behind when the astronauts returned to Earth.

APOLLO 15, 16 & 17
LUNAR OBJECT / 003
⌁

SCIENTIFIC EXPERIMENTS

Apollo astronauts deployed scientific instruments to study moonquakes, the lunar environment, and other properties of the Moon.

LUNAR SCIENCE
LUNAR OBJECT / 004
✣

LASER RANGING REFLECTORS

Reflector arrays placed during Apollo missions still support scientific measurements of the distance between Earth and the Moon.

STILL SCIENTIFICALLY USEFUL
02 / A CLOSER LOOK

THE APOLLO
ROVER.

The Lunar Roving Vehicle helped astronauts explore more of the Moon than they could reach on foot.

▱
ARCHIVE VISUAL / LRV
HUMAN EXPLORATION / 1971–1972

A VEHICLE
FOR ANOTHER WORLD.

The Lunar Roving Vehicle was used on Apollo 15, Apollo 16, and Apollo 17. It allowed astronauts to carry equipment and collect samples over greater distances.

After each mission, the rover remained near the landing site. Its story is part of the history of human exploration.
