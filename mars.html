MARS:
TRACES OF ARRIVAL.

Robotic explorers have crossed the vast distance to Mars. Their landers, landing systems, and other mission hardware tell the story of how we reached the Red Planet.

01 / MARTIAN LEGACY

WHAT REMAINS
ON MARS?

Landing on Mars is difficult. Spacecraft use several systems to slow down and reach the surface safely. Some of that hardware stays behind.

MARS OBJECT / 001
◉

HEAT SHIELDS

Heat shields protect spacecraft from the intense heating that occurs as they enter the Martian atmosphere. Some are released before landing and remain on Mars.

ENTRY SYSTEM
MARS OBJECT / 002
⌁

PARACHUTES

Parachutes help slow spacecraft during atmospheric descent. After deployment, they are left behind as the landing sequence continues.

DESCENT SYSTEM
MARS OBJECT / 003
⌖

LANDERS

Stationary landers conduct experiments and send information back to Earth. Many remain on the surface after their active missions end.

SURFACE SCIENCE
MARS OBJECT / 004

SKY CRANE HARDWARE

The Mars 2020 Perseverance landing system used a rocket-powered sky crane to lower the rover. The descent stage then flew away and landed at a distance.

02 / ROBOTIC EXPLORATION

THE ROVERS
AND THEIR JOURNEYS.

Rovers explore the Martian landscape, while the hardware that delivers them becomes part of the planet's exploration history.

1976

VIKING 1

One of the first successful spacecraft to operate on the Martian surface. Its lander conducted experiments and returned images and data.

2008

PHOENIX

A stationary lander that studied the Martian arctic environment, including water ice beneath the surface.

2012

CURIOSITY

The rover landed using a sky crane system and continues to study the geology and past habitability of Mars.

2021

PERSEVERANCE

The rover landed in Jezero Crater to explore ancient environments and collect samples for possible future return to Earth.
