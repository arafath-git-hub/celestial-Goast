IMPORTANT HISTORICAL MOMENTS

Journey Through the Years.

1969
MOON / APOLLO 11

Humanity Reaches the Moon

Apollo 11 successfully landed astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin on the Moon. The mission's lunar module descent stage remained on the lunar surface.

1971
MOON / APOLLO 15

The Lunar Roving Vehicle

Apollo 15 introduced the Lunar Roving Vehicle, allowing astronauts to travel farther across the lunar surface. The rover remains at the landing site.

1972
MOON / APOLLO 17

The Final Apollo Moon Landing

Apollo 17 became the last crewed Apollo mission to land on the Moon. Its equipment and lunar module descent stage remain part of the lunar historical record.

1997
MARS / PATHFINDER

Sojourner Explores Mars

NASA's Pathfinder mission delivered the Sojourner rover to Mars. It became the first successful Mars rover mission.

2004
MARS / SPIRIT & OPPORTUNITY

Two Rovers, One Red Planet

Spirit and Opportunity landed on Mars. Both rovers made important discoveries about the planet's geological history.

2010
MARS / SPIRIT

Spirit's Mission Ends

After becoming stuck in soft Martian soil, Spirit stopped communicating with Earth. NASA ended efforts to contact the rover in 2011.

2018
MARS / OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity's Long Journey Ends

Following a major global dust storm, Opportunity lost contact with Earth. NASA declared the mission complete in February 2019.

2021
MARS / PERSEVERANCE

A New Generation of Exploration

NASA's Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater. It continues humanity's search for clues about ancient Martian life.
